# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

*Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes.*

## 1. Working hours and remote work

Our core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in each employee's local time zone. Outside core hours, schedule your day in the way that works best for you and your team.

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with their manager's approval. Roles listed as "Remote" in the HR portal are fully remote. If you want to work from another country for more than 30 days in a calendar year, you need written approval from the People team, because this can create tax and immigration obligations for you and for the company.

Home office setup: every employee receives a one-time home office allowance of USD 400 for a desk, chair, monitor or similar equipment. Keep the receipts and submit them through the finance portal within 60 days of your start date.

## 2. Leave

### Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per year, accrued monthly. Up to five unused days carry over into the next year, and carried-over days must be used by 31 March. Request leave in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance; your manager approves it.

### Sick leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. If you are absent for more than two consecutive working days, upload a doctor's note to the HR portal. Sick days do not carry over.

### Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Please tell the People team at least 8 weeks before your expected start date so that we can plan cover for your work.

### Public holidays
Employees observe the public holidays of the country they are employed in. If you work on a public holiday because of an urgent customer issue, you receive a replacement day off to be taken within the following two months.

## 3. Expenses and travel

Submit business expenses with receipts within 30 days through the finance portal. Expenses over USD 500 need pre-approval from your manager before you spend the money.

During business travel, meals are covered up to USD 60 per day. Book economy class for flights under six hours; for longer flights, premium economy is allowed. Hotels should be booked through the company travel tool, which applies our negotiated rates. Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll run.

## 4. Learning and development

Each employee has a learning budget of USD 1,500 per calendar year for courses, books, certifications and conferences. Request it through the People team before you buy anything. Unused budget does not carry over to the next year. Employees may also spend up to four working hours per month on learning during work time.

## 5. Performance reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Each review includes a self-assessment, feedback from two peers chosen together with your manager, and a one-to-one conversation with your manager. Salary reviews take place once a year, in January, and are based on the December review.

## 6. Equipment and IT

Every employee receives a company laptop. Report a lost or stolen device to the IT team within 24 hours at it-help@northwind.example. Company data must only be stored on approved systems: the company cloud drive and the internal wiki. Personal USB drives are not allowed for company data.
